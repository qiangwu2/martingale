About the project

The Parisi formula identifies the thermodynamic limit of the free energy of the SK model through a variational principle. Talagrand's proof combines Gaussian integration by parts, recursive transforms, interpolation, and control of replica overlaps.

The formalization makes the analytic dependencies explicit: measurability, integrability, domination, continuity, differentiability, and endpoint identities all become part of the checked proof.

Current progress

The development follows Talagrand's proof in the SK model with exact covariance. Completed results have build-time axiom checks, while unfinished targets remain explicit.

Remaining work

The full Parisi formula is currently formalized modulo Theorem 2.2. The main remaining work is the interpolation and optimality analysis leading to coupled-replica overlap concentration. The subsequent convergence deduction is already represented in Lean.

The repository distinguishes completed theorems from conditional deductions and open placeholders, so a successful build is not presented as certification of unfinished results.

What Lean certifies

Lean checks the logical deduction once the definitions, theorem statements, and dependencies are fixed. Human mathematical review is still essential to confirm that the formal statements capture the intended model and theorem.
