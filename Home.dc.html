Qiang Wu

I am a Dunham Jackson Assistant Professor in the School of Mathematics at the University of Minnesota. My primary research interests are probability theory and statistical physics, with a particular focus on spin glasses.

I received my Ph.D. in mathematics from the University of Illinois at Urbana‑Champaign, advised by Partha S. Dey, and joined Minnesota in Fall 2023.
