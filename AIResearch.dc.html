Manuscripts in probability and statistical physics, grouped by how the proofs were developed.

AI-generated results

These proofs were generated almost entirely by AI. My role was primarily to prompt the models and request audits and revisions; I barely contributed mathematical insights or proof ideas. I take zero credit and ε responsibility for these results, as I have not had time to carefully check the proofs myself.

Several rounds of AI review found no obvious unresolved substantive errors or gaps within their scope. Due to limited computational resources, only parts have been verified in Lean, where available, conditional on stated assumptions.

AI-assisted results

These works build, to varying degrees, on my previous research. I contributed mathematical ideas and insights to guide the AI's proof development and checked the underlying proof ideas. This involvement gives me greater confidence in the results, though potential minor errors or gaps may remain.

The collection includes work on mixed p-spin free-energy fluctuations, joint parameter estimation, and approximate counting for spin glasses.
